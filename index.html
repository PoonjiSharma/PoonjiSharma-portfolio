Case Study 02 Preventive Health & Synchronized Audio Roadblock
4-Network Simultaneous Broadcast

World Heart Day: 360° Quad-Station Radio Roadblock & Conceptual Video Direction

The Strategic Challenge: Every hospital runs generic heart checkup ads on World Heart Day. Breaking through consumer fatigue required an unavoidable audio monopoly coupled with emotional visual narrative.

The Narrative Idea: Capture working professionals during the morning commute with a simultaneous 4-station radio takeover, shifting cardiovascular health from "a future fear" to "today's morning responsibility to your family".

360° Omnichannel Execution: Single-handedly orchestrated a synchronized broadcast roadblock across Red FM, Fever 104, Hit 95, and Big FM. Concurrently scripted and directed the on-ground emotional awareness video shoot featuring cardiologists and real clinical teams.

✦BEHIND THE WORK✦

Your Brand's Key to Better Marketing!

I didn't come into brand communication through a single, traditional path.

My background has been an energetic mix of media, storytelling, marketing, and working closely with people. From fast-paced newsrooms like NDTV and News NCR handling intense live reporting environments, to steering high-stakes campaigns, surgical robotics launches, and patient narratives in one of Asia's largest multispecialty hospitals, I've seen communication from fundamentally different perspectives.

Over time, I realised I thrive in the space where analytical thinking, precise messaging, and bold execution come together.

Working in healthcare branding has made this even more real. It's never just about making pretty creatives; I work with real human problems, real families, and communication that must drive responsible, life-changing action. That has made me hyper-conscious of clarity, intent, and accountability in everything I produce.

I'm comfortable working with data, structure, and operational planning, but I deeply care about how something feels and how audiences genuinely respond.
